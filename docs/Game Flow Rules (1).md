**If they choose NOT to keep it**

	A Fortune point is stored for later use. And if the roll would otherwise still succeed, than normal effects follow.

Fortune Points may be invoked before or after seeing the result of any roll. If possessed, they may be spent top replace any roll with a critical success. The action would resolve as if  normal critical success except in the situation that a **Critical Failure** is the roll being replaced.

**Misfortune Points** are gained whenever a critical failure is rolled during and skill check or attack, and the player is given the choice to accept or store it. If they accept it, the action resolves as written for a critical faiure, if they store it they store a misfortune point that GM notes down for the party, and it simply counts as a normal Failure.

If the player has a fortune point to spend, they mae replace a critical failure with a critical success, but this comes at the cost storing the misfortune point as well as spending the fortune point.  There is only one other way in which a combat roll may be re-rolled, this is through spending magicka.

**Magika Burst \-** You may spend a pint of Magika to re-roll any roll on your turn as long as you have magicka to spend. You may even take [Magicka burn](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mU6WFAAPm4dx2l3EvllKJKPisYMKlwSA-TUeJI4K1LI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.7f8ryi1ovaw4) damage to re-roll. But cannot re roll with if completely burned out.

These Magicka burst re-rolls DO NOT allow critical success. Any roll in a crit range simply counts as  normal roll if it would succeed. They however DO ALLOW critical failures on magicka re-rolls. Making this more dangerous the more frequently it is done. 

If using magika to re roll a critical failure, you accept the misfortune point, and roll again, risking another failure.

**GETTING HURT IN COMBAT**

Assuming an attack beats your ac and you do not dodge or otherwise avoided it you take damages.

First any DR from an ongoing magical effect would be applied if possible. Than yout take into account racial resistances (Dunmer fire resistant, nord cold resistant etc.) The remaining damage is dealt to your HP.  If that reduces it to zero or fewer points, YOu must immediately roll a complication which is applied immediately so. 

If you have any remaining spirit points (like structure, see how much I’m stealing from lancer too\!) your MP, AP, and HP are all rest to their new maximum, and the spirt point is spent. 

**Spirit points \-** You have ¼ level (rounded down) min 1 , spirit points. You regain one spirt point after a full nights rest, with food and in comfort. (you cannot be roughing ina shitty tent with thin stew) 

Once out of spirit points,  you're unconscious and dying and need another player to succeed a check to heal you. [Read here.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mU6WFAAPm4dx2l3EvllKJKPisYMKlwSA-TUeJI4K1LI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.xhxiwlg4vsd5)

**Complications \-**

These are cumulative wounds that you take over the course of play and are difficult to heal.   
To take a complication, roll 1d10. Than reduce that stat’s current Maximum by one. On a 1-3 reduce HP, a 4-6 reduce AP, a 7-9 reduce MP, and a 10 is a piece of readied/equipped, equipment or weapon of your choice. This equipment becomes damaged until fixed.
